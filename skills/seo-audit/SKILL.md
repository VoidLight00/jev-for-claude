Audit SEO for: $ARGUMENTS

Check separately:
- crawl and index signals: status, robots, canonical, sitemap references, redirects;
- page identity: title, description, one clear primary heading, language, structured data;
- content usefulness: search intent, unique answer, factual support, internal links, stale claims;
- sharing and discovery: Open Graph, social preview, image alt text;
- performance risks visible from available evidence.

For each finding provide evidence, impact, confidence, and the smallest fix. Distinguish source inspection from live rendered verification. Do not promise rankings or infer indexing from markup alone.
